6 Design Issues

  • Heavy filled pills for section titles compete with content
  • No entrance animation — abrupt pop-in feels unpolished
  • No visual connection between sidebar trigger and flyout
  • Items have no icon containers — icons feel naked and misaligned
  • No active route indicator inside the flyout
  • Flat single shadow — lacks depth and spatial hierarchy

6 Improvements

  • Dot + small-caps section labels — cleaner visual hierarchy
  • Animated shimmer accent bar at top ties to brand blue
  • Arrow notch visually anchors flyout to its sidebar trigger
  • Icon containers give items consistent weight and alignment
  • Active item highlighted with fill + trailing dot
  • Layered shadow creates depth; Esc hint improves discoverability

High No entrance animation

The flyout pops in with no transition. This makes the UI feel janky and unresponsive — the user can't track the element appearing.

Add: scale + translateX + opacity transition
High No active state

When a user is on Features, nothing in the flyout confirms that. The active page is invisible — users can't orient themselves.

Compare route.path to item.path in template
Med Heavy section pills

The filled pill (especially the dark #052659 one) dominates the visual field and competes with the navigation items themselves.

Replace with dot + small-caps label
Med No spatial anchor

The flyout floats free from its trigger. There's no visual connector (arrow/notch) showing which sidebar icon opened it — especially problematic in collapsed sidebar mode.

Add CSS ::before arrow pointing left
Low Flat icon presentation

Icons sit bare with no container. Adding a small rounded square background gives consistent optical weight and makes each item scannable at a glance.

30×30 rounded-lg container per icon
Low No keyboard hint

Power users expect Esc to close flyouts. Adding a small Esc badge in the footer is a subtle professional touch and improves a11y discoverability.

Footer: kbd[Esc] + "to close" hint
