# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Sacco staff and tellers are the primary users: officers at the counter processing deposits, withdrawals, loan applications and member registrations all day, often on shared machines in busy branch offices. Sacco managers and admins work alongside them, reviewing approvals, reports, chart-of-accounts and configuration. Members are a secondary audience through the self-service portal, checking their own savings, statements and loans. Platform (central) admins — the Mfuko Pro team — manage tenants, licenses and plans in a separate central surface.

## Product Purpose

Mfuko Pro is a multi-tenant SaaS platform that digitizes Savings and Credit Cooperative Organizations (Saccos): members, savings, loans, shares, groups and double-entry accounting, one isolated database per Sacco. Success means the books are always reconciled — statements, ledgers and balances that members and auditors can verify — tellers post transactions quickly with zero booking mistakes, and members trust their balances enough to self-serve instead of visiting the office.

## Positioning

A trustworthy digital ledger: accurate, auditable double-entry records a Sacco can trust over paper.

## Brand Personality

Modern, efficient, professional. A fast contemporary SaaS feel — competence expressed through speed and clarity, not gloss. The interface should feel like a serious financial tool a teller stops noticing after a week.

## Anti-references

Not legacy banking software: no cluttered gray form walls, tiny fonts, or 90s core-banking density. Not a generic admin template: no interchangeable Bootstrap/AdminLTE look with no identity.

## Design Principles

- **Every number is verifiable.** Balances, ledgers and statements always trace to their entries; the UI never shows a figure a user can't drill into or reconcile.
- **Speed at the counter.** Teller flows are keyboard-friendly, low-friction and interruption-safe; the fastest path is the default path.
- **Familiar over novel.** Standard affordances, one component vocabulary across all modules and both tenant and central surfaces; delight is saved for moments, not pages.
- **Density with hierarchy.** Financial data can run dense, but hierarchy (type scale, spacing, alignment) always makes the primary figure and next action obvious.
- **Trust through precision.** Aligned tabular figures, consistent formatting, exact language — precision in presentation signals precision in the books.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: ≥4.5:1 body-text contrast, full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation.
